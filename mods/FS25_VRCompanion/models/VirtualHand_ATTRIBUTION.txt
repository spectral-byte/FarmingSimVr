Changes made for FS25 VR (CC BY asks for changes to be indicated):

  * Exported to .i3d with the GIANTS Blender exporter. The model is a LEFT
    hand. The Companion Mod draws the left Virtual Hand as it is and the
    right Virtual Hand as its mirror image (see VRCompanionVirtualHands.lua).
  * In VirtualHandLeft.i3d: the externalShapesFile now names the shapes file
    that actually ships (VirtualHandLeft.i3d.shapes) and shadow casting of the
    shape is switched off (castsShadows="false"). The mesh (the .shapes file),
    the bone names and hierarchy, the bone positions and the materials are
    unchanged.
  * The rig was corrected. The GIANTS Blender exporter writes every bone's
    rotation 90 degrees short about X, which piles up down each finger: the
    first export composed to a skeleton about a fifth the size of the mesh.
    The bone rotations in VirtualHandLeft.i3d are now the armature's own
    parent-relative rotations, so the bones compose to the rest pose of the
    armature in "Gloved Hand LP.blend" (scripts/export_virtual_hand.py re-exports
    it; scripts/check_virtual_hand_rig.ps1 checks it).
  * The Companion Mod scales and turns the model onto the OpenXR grip pose
    at run time; nothing about that is stored in the files.
